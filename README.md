# Notipal and Mosaic

People pick up a Supernote because they want a quiet place to read and think on paper. Most of the time, the built-in Notes and Documents apps honor that well, right up until you find yourself digging through menus just to swap pens, or losing a sentence in your head while backing out to check another PDF.

Notipal is a floating toolbox plugin for Supernote. It grew up alongside Mosaic, a handwriting whiteboard plugin also made by me, Laumss. Almost every Notipal tool works right on the Mosaic canvas too.

Highlights

➤ Eight shared clipboards that remove the wall between Notes, Documents, Mosaic, and Sketch. Save handwriting, stickers, text boxes, links, and images, then paste them wherever you need them.
➤ LocalSend built in for quick Wi-Fi transfers with your phone, tablet, or computer. Move screenshots, recognized handwriting, web text, images, and documents back and forth, or sync all eight clipboard slots across two Supernotes.
➤ Smart Lasso that adapts to what you select. Lassoed handwriting goes out as recognized text, text boxes and document selections travel as clean text, and a link to another file sends the linked document itself. With nothing selected, draw around any part of the screen to crop it.
➤ Pen Slots, a slim vertical bar for switching pens, erasers, and the lasso in one tap, including thicker nibs and wider erasers than Supernote's stock menu.
➤ AI Relay, built around E Ink's pace. Ask about a handwritten paragraph, a cropped figure, or an equation, and watch the reply stream onto the page from RikkaHub on your phone or a local browser tab opened by scanning a QR code.

If you install Mosaic alongside Notipal, you also get:

➤ Quick Switch inside Notes and Documents. Swipe up from the bottom edge of any page to jump straight to recent notes, PDFs, or boards without opening Mosaic first.
➤ Sketch, a small scratch pad tucked inside Quick Switch that acts like a sticky note. Jot down a phone number or passing thought, or park a whiteboard card here to keep an outline or formula in view.
➤ Document screenshots and stitching. Crop passages from a PDF, stitch consecutive pages into a vertical strip or grid, and queue them for a note or send them straight to Mosaic.
➤ Read and write on top (Nomad and Manta). Float a translucent Mosaic board over a document, take notes inside a vertically scrolling Note Card, and turn the pages underneath with the side slider.

Quick Start
Search for Notipal and Mosaic in InkHub on your Supernote to install them directly.

What you need depends on what you plan to use:
➤ Toolbar, clipboards, pen slots, and screenshots only need a Supernote with plugin support.
➤ Sending and receiving files or text needs Wi-Fi and the free, open-source LocalSend app from F-droid, Google Play. Microsoft Store, App Store on your other device.
➤ AI Relay needs any browser or an Android phone running RikkaHub with a model provider API key on the same Wi-Fi.
➤ The whiteboard, Quick Switch, and Sketch come from the Mosaic plugin in InkHub.
➤ Exporting a PDF with your handwritten annotations baked in uses the DocuCraft plugin, also in InkHub.

You can open Notipal from the Note or Document's plugin tool menu, or tap Clipboard in any lasso menu to save a selection right away. On first launch, choose Always allow for file and network permissions so background tools keep working reliably.

---

The Toolbar
The toolbar pairs a main column of tools with a slim rail for your clipboard slots and a few quick controls. Drag it anywhere with your finger to keep it out of your writing path; tapping with your pen triggers tools without leaving stray ink underneath.

Its buttons shift depending on where you are. Insert Image and Doc Screenshot share the same spot, capturing the page while you read and dropping images in when you are writing in a note or on Mosaic. App-specific controls like Text Receive, Share Document, Ink Palette, and Note/Doc to Mosaic appear automatically where they fit. Optional tools (AI Relay and Insert Link) stay hidden until you turn them on in Settings.

Tap Collapse, or double tap the page with two fingers, to tuck the toolbar against the nearest edge as a small handle. Long press the handle to close Notipal completely. It also steps aside on its own whenever AI Relay, a Mosaic menu, Quick Switch, or Lexi is open.

Lexi is an upcoming plugin by Laumss that brings bilingual document translation, dictionary lookups, and Anki-style review into one place, running either full screen or as a compact floating window. Swipe a highlighter loosely across a few letters to look up the full word, or select whole sentences to translate them through Notipal's AI Relay.

Clipboards
Notipal gives you eight persistent clipboard slots along the side rail, shared across Notes, Documents, Mosaic, and Sketch.

In a note or document, lasso what you want, tap Clipboard in the lasso menu, and pick a slot. Handwriting, shapes, text boxes, images, and links all save cleanly (titles are the one exception). On Mosaic, tap a slot number directly to copy, or long press an empty slot to cut.

With nothing selected, tap any filled slot to paste. Notes accept everything saved. Documents accept handwriting and shapes only. On Mosaic, handwriting lands centered in view while text boxes and images become cards (links are skipped).

Clipboard Manager opens a visual grid of all eight slots so you can paste, inspect a slot full screen, or clear slots individually or all at once.

Pen Slots
Pen Slots puts a foldable, draggable vertical pen bar right on the page so you do not have to break your rhythm opening Supernote's pen menu. It holds up to nine slots, each set to a needle pen, ink pen, calligraphy pen, marker, eraser, or lasso with your choice of color and width. It also reaches wider than Supernote's stock menu (up to 3.0 for needle, 5.0 for ink and eraser, 6.0 for marker, and 7.5 for calligraphy).

Tap any slot to switch, or tap the active slot again to bounce back to whichever tool you were using right before. On Mosaic, the same bar switches your whiteboard pen using Ink Palette's nine presets.

Smart Lasso
Smart Lasso changes behavior based on whether you have an active selection.

When something is selected, it opens LocalSend to share it. Handwriting goes out as recognized text, text boxes and document selections travel as plain text, web links send the URL, and links to another note or document send the linked file itself.

With nothing selected, draw loosely around any area on screen to frame a crop. From there, send it over LocalSend, add it to Doc Screenshots for later, or drop it onto Mosaic as an image card. Long pressing Smart Lasso is a quick shortcut to sync all eight clipboards to another Supernote.

Document Screenshots and Insert Image
Reading deeply often means clipping diagrams, quotes, and tables as you go, then pulling them into a notebook later. In a document, tap Doc Screenshot to crop the current page, then pick Insert Next (queues it for one-tap placement in a note), Add to Queue (saves it under Screenshots), Send to Other Devices, or Stitch. Toggle Multi on if you want to cut several excerpts from the same page without closing the editor.

Stitching combines consecutive pages into either a tall vertical strip (with manual seam adjustment or Auto Align) or a neat grid, and lets you add, remove, or reverse pages on the fly.

In a note or on Mosaic, the same button becomes Insert Image. If you have crops queued, each tap drops the next one straight onto the page. Otherwise, it opens a browser sorted by Recent, Screenshots, From LocalSend, your system folders, and Trash. You can select multiple images to insert in order, or pick Crop and Insert on a single image to trim it and apply Original, Enhance, or B&W Text filters. Queued crops and incoming LocalSend images clean themselves up once inserted.

Text Receive
Sometimes typing a long quote, URL, or citation on a real keyboard beats writing it by hand. Tap Text Receive to drop a small target bubble on a Note or Mosaic page, drag it where you want, and send text over LocalSend from your phone or computer.

Incoming messages become stacked text boxes (or text cards on Mosaic) right at the bubble. If Text Receive is turned off, or if you are in a document or have another menu open, incoming text falls back to the system clipboard so you can still paste it anywhere. You can pick custom `.ttf` and `.otf` fonts from `/sdcard/Document/Fonts/` and set the default size in Settings.

Insert Link and Ink Palette
Insert Link is an optional tool for Notes that opens a modern file browser and drops native-styled links to your documents, with multi-select if you want to add several at once.

Ink Palette lives in the side rail on Mosaic and lets you restyle strokes you have already written. Lasso your strokes to open the full panel and adjust pen type, color, or width, or tap it with nothing selected to float a compact bubble of your nine presets. Every change works with Mosaic's undo history.

AI Relay
Running a full AI client directly on E Ink rarely feels good, so AI Relay lets a faster device on your desk do the heavy lifting while your Supernote handles prompting and typesetting. The actual model request runs on your phone or a computer browser over local Wi-Fi, with no Notipal server in the middle. When you pause to read or write, the connection rests to save battery and wakes right back up when you ask again.

AI Relay starts off by default. Turn it on under Optional Tools in Settings, or flip the permanent off switch on the same page if you want your tablet to stay strictly offline and AI-free.

There are two ways to connect:
➤ Pair with RikkaHub on Android (recommended). Once paired on the same Wi-Fi, questions pick up right inside the conversation open on your phone, voice prompts use your phone's mic, and Lexi and DocuCraft share the same connection.
➤ Host a local web page from the Supernote. Scan the QR code on a phone or open the address in a computer browser, then enter your API key (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint). Your provider settings stay saved on the Supernote.

You can type a question directly, pull recognized handwriting or document text from a selection, switch to Formula mode to attach handwritten math as a crisp image, crop any area on screen, or record voice via RikkaHub. Replies arrive with Markdown and LaTeX properly typeset. Expand the window, select the lines worth keeping, and tap Insert: prose lands as text boxes or cards, while equations are drawn directly onto the page as pen strokes. Keep the window floating, full screen, or tucked into a round bubble, with a searchable Inbox for past chats and favorites.

LocalSend, Share Document, and Clipboard Sync
LocalSend is a free, open-source app for Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, and Linux. Whenever the Notipal toolbar is open, your Supernote shows up on your local Wi-Fi as a nearby device.

Text sent to your Supernote goes to Text Receive or the clipboard. A single image sent while a note is open asks whether to insert it now or save it for later. Other files land in `/sdcard/INBOX`, and `.snplg` plugin packages go straight to `/sdcard/MyStyle`.

When sending from the Supernote, long press any device in the Send panel to set it as your default so single images and text snippets go straight there in one tap.

While reading, tap Share Document in the side rail to send the file over LocalSend. If it is an annotated PDF and DocuCraft is installed, Notipal exports a copy with your handwritten notes baked in, falling back to the original file if anything gets in the way. You can also tap Sync Clipboard (or long press Smart Lasso) to bundle all eight clipboard slots and send them to another Supernote.

Settings
Open Settings from Notipal's entry in the Supernote plugin manager to reorder toolbar buttons, toggle optional tools (or permanently turn AI off), pick a default LocalSend device, turn on 600 DPI outgoing screenshots, and choose the font and size for inserted text boxes.

# Mosaic

When a fixed-width notebook page runs out of room right as an idea starts branching out, Mosaic gives you an open handwriting whiteboard. Write anywhere, group strokes into cards, connect thoughts with bridges, and keep as many separate boards as your projects need.

Quick Switch and Sketch
With Mosaic installed, swipe up from the bottom edge of any note, document, or board to open a drawer with two tabs: Recent and Sketch.

Recent lists your boards alongside recent notes and PDFs (showing covers and reading progress), so you can jump straight back to any page or start a new board without going through the file manager.

Sketch is a small handwriting pad inside the same drawer that works like a sticky note on the corner of your desk. It has its own mini-toolbar (three pens, eraser, and lasso), saves automatically, and ties into Notipal's shared clipboard at native note scale. Through the Card Box, you can copy any whiteboard card into Sketch to keep a formula sheet or outline one swipe away, or promote your Sketch notes onto a board as a text card.

Notipal on the Whiteboard
Start Notipal while Mosaic is open and the toolbar adapts automatically, working both on the open canvas and inside Note Cards. Your eight clipboards move freely between notes, documents, Sketch, and boards. Screenshots, Insert Image, Text Receive, and AI Relay land directly as image or text cards. Pen Slots and Ink Palette drive whiteboard ink.

You can also tap Note to Mosaic or Doc to Mosaic in the side rail of a note or PDF to pull selected pages onto a board. Note pages merge into a Note Card, and PDF pages render at 600 DPI into Document Cards with your handwritten notes and highlights still editable.

Read and Write on Top (Nomad and Manta)
Exclusively on the Nomad and Manta, you can layer Mosaic directly over a document to read and take long-form notes in one view.

Turn on Board translucency in the side menu and dial the opacity between 10% and 80% so the page underneath shows through; inside a Note Card, a subtle white backing keeps your handwriting crisp over dense print. Enable Slider page turn right beneath it to flip underlying document pages with the hardware side slider while Mosaic stays open. You can also turn on Page turn mode for discrete E Ink-friendly viewport jumps and single-step pinch zoom instead of continuous panning.

A setup worth trying: open a PDF, launch Mosaic with translucency and slider page turn enabled, create a Note Card, and open it. You get an endlessly scrolling vertical sheet for notes while flicking the side slider to turn book pages underneath, much like a reMarkable.

Whiteboard
A compact, draggable capsule (horizontal or vertical) holds four pen slots, an eraser, and a lasso, while the side menu handles zoom, undo and redo, boards, Card Box, backup, templates, translucency, and page turn mode. Each pen slot can hold a calligraphy pen, fineliner, marker, or a pencil with paper grain and tilt response.

The canvas, every card, and every bridge act as independent writing surfaces with palm rejection and automatic white ink over dark backgrounds. There is no separate shape tool: pause your pen for half a second at the end of a stroke to snap clean lines, triangles, ellipses, or polygons, with optional geometry snapping (square, golden ratio, A-paper, equilateral, right-angled, and more).

Working with cards stays gesture-driven. Draw a rough rectangle around strokes and pause to wrap them into a card, pull a stroke out from inside a card to spawn a connected one, draw a bracket along an edge to stretch it, or lasso handwriting to convert it into a typed text card (where the marker works as a smart text highlighter). Slide same-color cards together to link them with flexible bridges, write on a bridge to lock a group, or draw a thin connecting thread between any two cards.

Note Cards open into tall vertical notebook pages, Document Cards hold imported PDF pages with editable annotations, and Image Cards hold pictures and screenshots. Whenever you open Mosaic from a note, lasso any area and tap Insert into note to drop in a tagged snapshot that refreshes automatically when you close Mosaic and jumps straight back to that spot when tapped.

Boards, Card Box, and Backup
Create as many boards as you need, either blank or generated straight from a note or PDF (non-PDF documents can convert through DocuCraft first). Each board remembers its own cards, template, translucency, and viewport. Use Card Box to carry cards between boards, including your Sketch pad. Boards save automatically, and you can export or import backups from `/sdcard/EXPORT/Notipal/mosaic/`.

In Development: Web Sync and Inkflow

Everything in this last section is still in development. Early builds are running and being tuned, though they are not yet in release builds.

Web sync pairs Mosaic with a companion whiteboard in a desktop browser, either over your local network or through a shareable online room link. Strokes, cards, and connections sync live in both directions with simultaneous editing and the same pen gestures on both screens.

Inkflow turns your Supernote into a pressure- and tilt-sensitive USB drawing tablet wired to the web whiteboard on your computer, complete with hover tracking. Select a passage in a document to send it to your computer's board as a text card, while a control window across the lower half of the screen lets you switch tools, pan and zoom the desktop canvas, and flip document pages without putting your pen down.
